MORE THAN A FESTIVAL

Where Tradition Comes Alive.

Durga Puja is more than a celebration. It is a time when families, friends and communities come together in the spirit of devotion and joy.

Our Puja brings together generations, traditions and people from different walks of life to celebrate the arrival of Maa Durga.

From the first preparations to the final farewell, every moment is created with faith, dedication and love.
