A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Arav Sharma.

I'm a 13-year-old boy who loves building websites and bringing creative ideas to life.

This Durga Puja 2026 website represents fifteen days of dedication, learning, problem-solving, and creativity. What started as an idea became a project where I could explore web development and create something meaningful for my community.

From designing pages and writing code to organising events and bringing photographs together in a gallery, every part of this journey has taught me something new.

Of course, not everything worked perfectly the first time. There were bugs to fix, designs to improve, and plenty of things to learn. But every challenge became another opportunity to make the website better.

Still learning. Always building.
